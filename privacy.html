Related: Terms of Service

2. Data stored on your device

Pickleball Rules Quiz stores game data only in your browser's localStorage to make the game work. This includes progress, scores, mistakes, game history, feature states, and device-local full access activation data.

This data:

  • never leaves your device automatically
  • is not sent to our servers
  • can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser storage

Legal basis: performance of the service (Article 6.1.b GDPR).

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay, Stripe collects data such as your email address, payment details, IP address, and device information.

Stripe acts under its own privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy

Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6.1.b GDPR).
Stripe may transfer data outside the EU using Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Emails and optional forms

If you contact us, choose to share gameplay statistics by email, or submit an optional signup form, we receive your email address and any content you send.

These actions are voluntary. Any optional form may be provided by a third-party email platform acting under its own terms and privacy policy.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6.1.a GDPR).

5. Data retention

Game data stored in your browser remains until you delete it. Emails are kept only as long as necessary to handle your request.

6. Cookies and tracking

We use GoatCounter for privacy-friendly website analytics. GoatCounter does not use tracking cookies and does not create persistent visitor profiles. We use it only to understand basic page traffic and improve the service.

Pickleball Rules Quiz does not use advertising trackers. Stripe may set cookies during checkout under its own responsibility.

Our hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address and timestamp, for security and reliability purposes only.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, and to object to processing where applicable.

To exercise your rights, contact: Contact us

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr.

Pickleball Rules Quiz stocke les données de jeu uniquement dans le localStorage de votre navigateur pour faire fonctionner le jeu. Ces données :

Stripe agit sous sa propre politique de confidentialité : https://stripe.com/privacy
