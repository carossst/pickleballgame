What this quiz covers

The 2026 questions target the changes you will actually meet on court. The volley serve's upward arc now applies to the paddle, not the arm. The non-volley zone rules moved from Section 9 to Section 11, with the act of volleying given a precise definition. The net post is no longer an automatic rally-ender the moment the ball touches it — the sequence matters. In rally scoring, the receiving side can now win the game on its game point. Spectators shall not be consulted on calls — the soft "should" is gone. Time-outs must be called clearly by voice, signal, or both, and a time-out called after the serve is a fault. Even the extra ball in your pocket has a rule now.

Each card states the 2026 rule as it is written today, with the new rule number — because half the confusion this season is players citing last year's map.

Common questions

What is the biggest rule change in the 2026 pickleball rulebook?

For everyday play, the volley serve's upward arc test moving from the arm to the paddle (Rule 7.C.1) — it outlaws the flat serve. Structurally, the entire rulebook was renumbered, so most rules now live at new references.

Did the kitchen rules change in 2026?

The substance is stable, but the rules moved from Section 9 to Section 11 and the act of volleying received a precise definition. If you cite kitchen rules by number, the old 9.x references are now wrong.
